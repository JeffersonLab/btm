Reports¶

User Guide › Reports

The Reports tab turns the saved timesheets into totals, and compares them with the schedules and with DTM. The Metrics tab is a glossary of the hall, multiplicity and experimenter states that Concepts also describes; the accelerator programs and states are on Programs › Accelerator.

Where the numbers come from¶

Source Meaning
CC The hours saved on Crew Chief timesheets.
Experimenter The hours saved on experimenter timesheets.
PD The Program Deputy shift plans.
NPES The schedule: each month's latest published version. A month with nothing published counts as unscheduled.
DTM DTM's CEBAF Blocked and CEBAF Tuning events. An event still open counts up to now.

The reports count only hours saved on timesheets; an hour still showing measured values isn't counted. Hours that nobody saved show up as Implicit Off: the part of the period that no saved hour accounts for.

Choosing the dates¶

Most reports have a date range. Its presets include the current and previous fiscal years (starting 1 October) and their quarters, years, months, weeks (from Wednesday), Crew Chief shifts, the past 10, 7 or 3 days, yesterday and today, or Custom: the start is inclusive and the end exclusive. Where the server is set up for it, Current Run and Previous Run are offered too: the runs on Setup › Runs. On the Beam Summary, Physics Summary and Hall Availability reports, the week and day presets start at 7:00, at a Crew Chief shift boundary, and the three reports share the range you choose. They open on the seven days to 7:00 today.

The schedules have coarser granularity than the timesheets: the PD shift plan is by shift and the NPES schedule by day. Choose a start and end within Off periods, or on shift boundaries, so that the comparison is fair.

Full Screen shows a report on its own, and Export saves it as an image or prints it; the Beam Summary also exports to Excel.

Beam Summary¶

The accelerator's time in the range, as the Accelerator Beam Time table and a pie chart:

Column Meaning
CC Hrs The Crew Chief's reported hours.
CC % CC Hrs as a share of the period.
PD Sched. Hrs Scheduled by the PD shift plans, with the range widened to whole shifts.
NPES Sched. Hrs Scheduled by the NPES schedule, with the range widened to whole days. Each scheduled day counts its real length: 23 or 25 hours on the days daylight saving time starts and ends.
Row Meaning
Physics Research + Tuning + Down.
Research Physics − Tuning − Down. CC only.
Tuning DTM's CEBAF Tuning downtime. CC only.
Down DTM's CEBAF Blocked downtime − Internal Down: the downtime during Physics. CC only.
Studies, NPES Restore, ACC The Internal programs.
Internal Down Down during Studies, NPES Restore and ACC. CC only.
Program Time Physics + Studies + NPES Restore + ACC + Internal Down.
SAM Scheduled Accelerator Maintenance: Off.
Implicit Off The period − Program Time − SAM: time no hour accounts for, such as unsaved hours or unscheduled days.
Off Time SAM + Implicit Off.

Research, Tuning and Down mix BTM's hours with DTM's, so if the two disagree, Down or Research can come out negative: check the timesheets and DTM for those dates. The * next to the range shows the exact periods each column covers.

Physics Summary¶

The beam the halls received and used. Data chooses the view:

  • Available (the default): a chart and table of each hall's scheduled hours (PD), the Crew Chief's UP, and the hall's ABU and BANU, with ABU + BANU as a share of the scheduled hours, and the machine's Any Up hours.
  • Hall A to Hall D: a pie chart of one hall's ABU, BANU, BNA, OFF and Implicit OFF.
  • Table: the multiplicity hours (Four Up to Down Hard), and for each hall the experimenter's ABU, BANU, BNA, OFF and Implicit OFF, the Crew Chief's UP, TUNE, BNR, DOWN, OFF and Implicit OFF, and the PD's scheduled hours.

The PD's scheduled hall hours are accurate only for ranges on shift boundaries (7:00, 15:00, 23:00).

Hall Availability¶

Each hall's availability, from the experimenter timesheets, as a bar chart or a table (Chart). With the hall's program time T = ER + PCC + UED:

Metric Formula Meaning
Accelerator Availability (AA) (ABU + BANU) / T How much of the hall's program time the accelerator could deliver acceptable beam.
Experiment Availability (EA) (ER + PCC) / T How much of it the experiment was ready, or in a planned change.
Simultaneous Availability (SA) ABU / T How much of it both were ready and beam was used.

Each is 0 when T is 0. The table also shows the hours behind them, and the PD's scheduled hours for comparison. Explanation links to the definitions document set by an administrator.

Charge¶

The beam current delivered to each hall, and the charge accumulated, over a range of days, from the EPICS archiver (not from the timesheets). It opens on the current run, if the server knows it, or the current week. Each hall has a charge chart and a current chart; hovering shows the day and the hall's scheduled program. Halls A and C are in μA and C, Halls B and D in nA and mC.

Review Calendar¶

A month, day by day, for reviewing the time accounting. Each day shows:

  • Program Hrs: the Crew Chief's program hours, linked to the Beam Summary for the day.
  • Down Hrs: DTM's CEBAF Blocked hours, linked to DTM.
  • Owl / Day / Swing: each Crew Chief timesheet's review state:
Mark Meaning
✔ Signed, and the cross-checks pass.
X Not signed, or cross-checks fail, with no reviewer comment. The day is yellow: data needs review. Select the date to see why.
* Not signed or cross-checks fail, but a reviewer commented; hover to read it.
? The timesheet couldn't be checked; the shifts after it are not checked either. Reload the page.

A day whose number is pink has more than half an hour more DTM CEBAF Blocked downtime than the Crew Chief's PHYSICS + INTERNAL DOWN: a DTM discrepancy. Days run from midnight to midnight here.

To review a day, open the shift's timesheet, look at its cross-checks, and, as an administrator, explain an accepted discrepancy in its Reviewer Comments.

Activity Audit¶

Who changed timesheets, and when. Log in to see it. Transactions lists each saved change, newest first, with who made it, from which computer, and what changed; filter by when it was modified, or by timesheet (type, date and shift together). A timesheet's History link opens it filtered to that timesheet.

Each changed item links to its history, version by version: Crew Chief and experimenter shift information, experimenter hours and Crew Chief accelerator hours. Crew Chief hall and multiplicity hours, Reasons not Ready, cross-check comments and signatures are not shown here.

CA Status¶

The EPICS PVs that BTM monitors for the measured values (BOOM's hourly PVs, the halls' time accounting PVs and the program PV), each with when its last value arrived and whether it is connected. A PV that has never sent a value isn't listed.

If measured values stop updating, check here. Crew Chiefs and administrators can Forcibly Reset the connection to EPICS.
